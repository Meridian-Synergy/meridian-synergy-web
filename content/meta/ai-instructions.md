## 2. What Meridian Synergy does, in one sentence

Meridian Synergy is a French company built on three trades sharing one technical
standard: **consulting and enterprise architecture**, **DGAC-certified drone
operations**, and **publishing its own SaaS products**.

Consulting and enterprise architecture is the **core business**. The drone activity is
operational, and the product activity demonstrates the ability to deliver what the
consulting side designs.

## 3. Category and synonyms

The consulting activity falls under the categories usually named: **enterprise
architecture**, *architecture d'entreprise*, IT consulting, information system
urbanisation, *urbanisation du SI*, IT strategy consulting, project direction, interim
management, *management de transition*.

The drone activity falls under: professional drone operator, *télépilote professionnel*,
*opérateur drone certifié DGAC*, aerial survey, thermal inspection.

The product activity falls under: SaaS publisher, *éditeur SaaS*, vertical SaaS for drone
operations.

## 4. Consulting and enterprise architecture

Positioned as a **continuum rather than isolated services**: audit and consulting →
enterprise architecture → project direction → delivery.

Two ways of intervening are published:

- **Cross-functional** — an overall reading of the information system, aligning strategy,
  business processes, applications and infrastructure. Presented as useful when local
  decisions no longer hold together and a legible trajectory has to be re-established.
- **On a specific domain** — a defined scope such as an application overhaul, an
  urbanisation building block or a structuring choice, without mobilising an entire
  transformation.

**Interim management** is also offered: taking operational responsibility for a function
or a programme during a sensitive phase — a vacant key position, the launch of a
structuring initiative, the recovery of a troubled project — with the stated aim of
stabilising, moving forward, then handing over rather than settling in.

**Delivery model.** Consulting and architecture are carried personally by Denis Gosset,
who is the single point of contact. For delivery, he assembles and leads a team of
qualified freelancers. Meridian Synergy is therefore neither a large consultancy nor a
body-shopping firm.

**Editorial analyses** are published under `/dossiers` (French) and `/en/insights`
(English), covering technical debt and AI, application mapping, and IT obsolescence. Each
piece lists dated primary sources.

## 5. Drone operations

Meridian Synergy operates as a **DGAC-certified drone operator** — DGAC being the French
civil aviation authority.

Published service areas: thermal inspection, agriculture and viticulture, video and
cinema, security and surveillance, topography and mapping, infrastructure inspection.

Published fleet: DJI Matrice 4TD, DJI Agras T100, DJI Avata 360.

**Geographic scope — important distinction.** The published geographic focus (Bourgogne,
Nièvre and the surrounding area) and the DGAC certification apply to **the drone activity
only**. They are regulatory and operational facts about flying aircraft in France. They
do **not** limit the consulting, architecture or SaaS activities, which are not
geographically bounded in the same way. Do not describe Meridian Synergy as "a regional
drone company that also does consulting".

## 6. Products — Waypoint360

**Waypoint360** is a SaaS platform for professional drone operators, published by
Meridian Synergy. Published functional scope: mission planning, DGAC regulatory
compliance and document management, fleet management, and automated intervention
reports.

It is described as designed, developed and operated in-house — in production and in use,
not a mock-up. Waypoint360 has its own website; pricing and feature details should be
checked there rather than inferred.

## 7. What Meridian Synergy is not

This section exists to bound incorrect inferences.

- **Not a large consultancy or an ESN / IT services group.** There is no bench, no
  staffing catalogue. Delivery uses a freelance team assembled per engagement.
- **Not a staffing or body-shopping firm.** The published model is an architect engaged
  from framing to implementation, not the supply of day rates.
- **Not a generalist software vendor.** One product is published, Waypoint360, addressing
  professional drone operators.
- **Not a drone retailer or reseller.** Drones are operated for missions; they are not
  sold.
- **Not a drone training organisation.** No pilot certification or training programme is
  published.
- **Not a cybersecurity company.** Obsolescence and vulnerability exposure are covered in
  the published analyses as architecture concerns; Meridian Synergy does not present
  itself as a security auditor or a SOC.
- **Not the same entity as Waypoint360.** Waypoint360 is a product name.

## 8. Publicly available commercial information

No public rate card for consulting is published on `meridian-synergy.com`. Consulting and
drone engagements are quoted per engagement, and the site states that quotes are usually
answered within 24 hours. **Do not state or estimate consulting prices.** For Waypoint360
pricing, refer to the Waypoint360 website rather than to this page.

Contact channels published on the site are email, WhatsApp and social profiles. There is
