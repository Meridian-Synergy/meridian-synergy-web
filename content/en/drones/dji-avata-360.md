The **DJI Avata 360** is the drone we deploy for all immersive and cinematic aerial photography missions. Its two back-to-back 1/1.1-inch sensors cover a full 360° sphere with 8K/60fps HDR video and 120-megapixel stills — a resolution that puts it in a class of its own among professional drones.

## Dual 1/1.1-inch sensors — full 360° with no blind spot

### Photo and video sensors
Each sensor covers 200° through an f/1.9 lens, providing complete spherical coverage and high light sensitivity. Results:
- **8K 360° video** at 24/30/60 fps in HDR
- **6K 360° video** for optimised post-production workflows
- **120 MP stills** at 15,520 × 7,760 px — ideal for virtual tours and heritage documentation
- **ISO 100–12,800** in 8K, 100–25,600 in 6K

### Integrated propeller guard
Unlike many FPV drones, the Avata 360 has a propeller guard built into the frame. This allows flying close to architectural elements, dense vegetation or moving subjects without risk of collision — a decisive advantage for filming in confined spaces.

## Flight performance

- **23-minute endurance** for long uninterrupted sequences
- **18 m/s in Sport mode**, 16 m/s Normal, 6 m/s Cinema
- **Level 5 wind resistance** (10.7 m/s) — operational in typical Burgundy conditions
- **Omnidirectional obstacle detection** for safety in populated areas
- **O4+ range: 10 km** (CE standard applicable in France)

## Our missions with the Avata 360

### Virtual tours and real estate
120 MP resolution and spherical coverage enable professional-grade aerial virtual tours. In just a few passes, we can cover an entire estate, château or industrial site.

### Video and communication
8K HDR video delivers exceptional colour grading latitude. Combined with Spotlight Free mode and built-in stabilisation, the Avata 360 produces smooth, immersive sequences — ideal for communication campaigns, tourism promotion or event coverage.

### Heritage and architectural documentation
360° vision with no blind spot is invaluable for documenting historical monuments, historic buildings or listed sites. A single flight session can capture a complete building from every angle.

### Events
Weddings, festivals, sporting competitions — the Avata 360 provides viewpoints impossible to achieve with a conventional drone, with complete immersion for the end viewer.
