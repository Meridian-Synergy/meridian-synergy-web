The **DJI Matrice 4TD** combines thermal infrared and triple visible optics for effective surveillance day and night. Our **CATS** certification (DGAC Specific category) authorises beyond-line-of-sight operations and degraded conditions — essential for professional security missions.

## Why use drones for security and surveillance?

A surveillance drone covers in minutes what would take hours to inspect on foot. It delivers:

- **Night thermal vision**: detection of human or animal presence in total darkness
- **Extended coverage**: up to 25 km transmission range, 49 minutes endurance
- **Discretion**: rapid deployment without alerting, capture from a safe distance
- **Documentation**: timestamped, geolocated recording of every observed event

## Concrete applications

### Event security
Monitoring crowd flows at concerts, Christmas markets, sporting events or cultural gatherings. The drone identifies pressure zones or emerging incidents before they escalate, coordinated with ground teams.

### Industrial and agricultural site surveillance
Automated perimeter patrols over warehouses, farm buildings, silos. Real-time detection of intrusions, fire outbreaks or vandalism. Particularly suited to large agricultural or wine estates during harvest season.

### Search and rescue
In coordination with local authorities (gendarmerie, SDIS), the thermal drone quickly locates a missing person in woodland, a maize field or difficult terrain — where ground searches can take hours. The Morvan and Nièvre forests make this a frequent operational need.

### Environmental monitoring and anti-poaching
Surveillance of sensitive areas (Natura 2000 zones, reservoirs, state forests), detection of offences (fly-tipping, poaching) in coordination with wildlife wardens and OFB agents. Thermal imaging detects vehicles or individuals even under tree cover.

### Early fire detection
During drought periods, monitoring of roadsides, forest edges and mandatory firebreak zones. The thermal sensor detects abnormal heat sources well before a fire becomes visible to the naked eye.

## Who is it for?

- **Event organisers** (associations, municipalities, production companies) wanting a supplementary surveillance capability
- **Industrial security managers** (quarries, logistics platforms, power plants)
- **Farmers and wine estates** protecting their harvests and facilities
- **Gendarmerie and SDIS** for search and rescue operations (subject to protocol)
- **Private security companies** looking to add drone capability to their service offering

## When to intervene?

- **Events**: deployment timed to peak risk moments (entry/exit, evening)
- **Night surveillance**: thermal camera is optimal at night, with or without moonlight
- **Preventive patrols**: regular schedule agreed with the client (daily, weekly)
- **Emergencies**: mobilisation within 2 hours for unplanned interventions in Nièvre and neighbouring departments
