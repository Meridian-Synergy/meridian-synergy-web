The **DJI Matrice 4TD** features a triple lens system — wide-angle 24 mm, telephoto 70 mm and super-telephoto 168 mm — all at 48 megapixels and 4K 30fps video. A professional tool combining enterprise-grade stability and the image quality demanded for communication, tourism and real estate.

## Why choose drone aerial photography?

Traditional aerial photography (helicopter, light aircraft, crane) is out of reach for most budgets. Drones change that:

- **Cinematic quality** at controlled cost
- **Flexibility**: custom flight paths, angles impossible any other way
- **Rapid deployment**: operational in under 15 minutes
- **Triple optics**: from sweeping wide-angle panoramas to discreet super-telephoto, without changing equipment

## Concrete applications

### Tourism and destination marketing
Burgundy is one of France's most photographed regions: UNESCO World Heritage vineyards (Côte de Nuits, Côte de Beaune), Canal de Bourgogne, Morvan forest, medieval châteaux. Aerial imagery showcases these landscapes for tourism offices, local authorities and accommodation providers.

### Real estate and land development
Present properties at their best: overview of a wine estate, positioning of a building plot relative to amenities, volumetrics of an industrial building. Buyers and investors decide faster with aerial visuals.

### Events and live capture
Weddings at Burgundy properties, cycling events, outdoor concerts, regional fairs: the drone captures what ground cameras cannot, discreetly thanks to its low noise level.

### Institutional and corporate communication
Construction progress shots for project owners, site plans for town planning documents, industrial site presentations for investors — professional aerial imagery adds value to your projects and infrastructure.

### Wine estates and agriculture
Aerial tours of vineyards for marketing materials, documentation of planting or winery renovation works, presentation videos for wine merchants and restaurants.

## When to intervene?

- **Golden hour** (sunrise and sunset) for warm, high-contrast images — preferred for viticulture and heritage
- **Spring and autumn** in Burgundy: colourful vegetation, vines in flower or autumn foliage
- **Systematic weather planning**: we check forecasts up to 48 hours in advance and reschedule if conditions are not optimal
- **Events**: deployment timed to the programme, set up discreetly in under 15 minutes
