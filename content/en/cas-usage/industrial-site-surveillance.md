High-risk industrial sites — Seveso sites, chemical platforms, energy production facilities, large logistics installations — have specific security requirements that fixed systems (CCTV cameras, guard patrols) cover imperfectly. The drone complements these systems by providing mobile, responsive and thermal surveillance: it patrols perimeters, inspects zones inaccessible from the ground, and detects intrusions or process anomalies in real time.

## Specific security challenges on industrial sites

Industrial sites present several characteristics that complicate their surveillance:

- **Large perimeters** — major industrial platforms extend across several tens of hectares, making exhaustive perimeter surveillance by ground security patrols very costly
- **Restricted access zones** — certain areas (tanks, process zones, storage) are inaccessible to security teams without specific procedures
- **Malicious activity and intrusion risks** — material theft, sabotage, unauthorised access to hazardous zones
- **Process anomaly detection** — steam leaks, overheating, outdoor fire ignition

## What the drone contributes to industrial security

### Perimeter patrol

The drone covers the site perimeter according to a programmed or on-demand flight plan, complementing guard patrols. Its speed (up to 15 m/s), relative discretion and aerial vantage point allow it to detect intrusions that fixed cameras would miss (blind spots, masking vegetation).

### Inspection of inaccessible zones

Certain parts of an industrial site are difficult to control from the ground: roofs of large industrial buildings, elevated storage zones, retention pond banks, confined spaces between equipment. The drone inspects them without exposing personnel to risk.

### Night-time thermal surveillance

The drone's thermal camera detects the body heat of intruders at range, by night or by day. It also identifies fire ignition points (hot spots on a roof, smoke visible thermally before it becomes visible optically) and hot fluid leaks.

### Alarm response

When a perimeter alarm is triggered (motion detector, fence vibration sensor), the drone can be deployed in 1 to 2 minutes to visually verify the zone before committing intervention teams. This time saving reduces costly false alarms and improves reactivity in the event of a real incident.

## Seveso regulatory context

Seveso-classified sites (lower and upper tier) are subject to enhanced security obligations under environmental law. The use of drones for security purposes on these sites must be integrated into the Risk Prevention Plan (PPR) and comply with the site's internal procedures (flight permits, prohibited zones, coordination with HSE teams).

Our pilots are trained in working protocols for high-risk industrial sites and operate in coordination with HSE managers and facility security teams.
