I produce a full diagnosis of your site, hand it to you, and you do what you like
with it — including giving it to another provider. That is written on the first
page of the document.

This is not generosity, it is a way of working: I would rather a conversation start
from measured facts than from a brochure.

## What the document contains

Findings, each built the same way: **what was measured**, **what it changes for
you**, **what to do about it**. Every point carries a label — critical, important,
minor, or compliant.

What already works comes first. A document that only lists shortcomings reads as an
indictment, and stops being believed before the end.

The sections vary with your activity, but most often include:

- **Visibility** — what search engines actually see of your pages, and what AI
  engines see, which execute no JavaScript.
- **Business profile** — presence, primary category, consistency between the
  profile and your site, reviews left unanswered. For a local business this is
  often the most profitable lever.
- **Off-site presence** — whether your social accounts link back to the site, and
  whether the web can tell they refer to the same company.
- **Performance** — the real weight of your pages and the wait a visitor faces on a
  phone, not on your desktop over fibre.
- **Protection of your domain name** — who can send an email pretending to be you.
  Across eight construction companies I measured in the Nièvre, **none** was
  protected.
- **Compliance** — cookie banner, legal notice, and above all the gap between what
  they announce and what the site actually does.
- **Accessibility and contrast** — measured on the rendered pixel.

## What this document is not

It is **not a security audit**. I run no intrusion test, no vulnerability scan, no
search for weaknesses. Everything rests on what is public: your pages, your
headers, your domain records, search engines.

Nor is it a disguised quote. Recommended actions are grouped by effort, never
priced in days, and nothing obliges you to hand them to me.

The closing chapter states what was examined **and what was not**, with the reason.

## How it works

You have nothing to provide: no access, no password, no preliminary meeting. I work
from what is public.

You receive an online document and its printable PDF equivalent, readable offline.
It is presented in your colours as much as in mine, and a button switches between
the two.

If the findings call for a follow-up, I propose a **redesign trajectory**: what to
rework, in what order, and what can wait. It is a proposal, not a condition.
